STRIDE LOGO KIT
===============
Brand coral: #FF5436

/svg — master vector files (infinitely scalable)
  stride-mark-primary.svg     Coral circle, white bolt — default mark
  stride-mark-reversed.svg    White circle, coral bolt — for coral/dark backgrounds
  stride-mark-mono-black.svg  Black circle, white bolt — single-colour print
  stride-mark-mono-white.svg  White circle, black bolt — single-colour print
  stride-bolt-*.svg           Bolt glyph only (coral / white / black), transparent

/png — transparent-background exports
  stride-mark-primary at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 180, 192, 256, 512, 1024 px
  variants at 512 and 1024 px
  bolt-only at 512 px

/favicon
  favicon.ico            multi-size (16/32/48) for browsers
  apple-touch-icon.png   180px on white, for iOS home screen

Usage notes:
- 512 & 1024 = app icons, social profile pictures (Instagram, LinkedIn, X)
- 192 & 512 = Android / PWA manifest icons
- 16–48 = favicons and UI chrome
- Use the SVGs anywhere the platform accepts them (web, print, signage)
